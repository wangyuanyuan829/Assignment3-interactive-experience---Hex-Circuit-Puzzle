## 1. Starting Idea
The brief requires one carefully designed interactive experience with an input-response-feedback loop.
I was inspired by a hex grid circuit puzzle. The core idea: users click hexagonal tiles to rotate pipes and build a complete electrical circuit connecting the power source to all light bulbs.
Every click is a deliberate user input. The program rotates the pipe and checks circuit connectivity immediately. Users see the pipe change visually and can continue adjusting tiles until all bulbs light up.
